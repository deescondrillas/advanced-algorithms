Dear Grandma,

Yesterday I found the blue notebook you gave me. Between its pages were a recipe, a shopping list, and a drawing of a house with enormous windows. It made me happy to recognize your handwriting and remember the afternoons when we cooked without paying much attention to the clock.

Today I tried to make bread by following your instructions. The dough was sticky at first, and I thought I had made a mistake. I waited a few minutes, added a little flour, and continued kneading. While the bread was in the oven, I opened the window to listen to the birds in the garden.

It was not exactly like yours, but we shared several slices at dinner. I kept a photograph and wrote down what I would change next time. When you visit, we can try again together. I have many questions and would love to hear your stories.

With love,
Lucy.
